# When Dance Becomes Data 💃📊

### What Can Technology Measure, and What Does It Miss?

**A creative exploration of dance, human expression, and Data Engineering.**

Presented on November 5, 2026, as part of my Data Engineering learning journey.

Dance has always been close to my heart. Through this presentation, I wanted to explore something personal: what happens when creativity meets technology?

We experience dance through rhythm, movement, expression, and human connection. But a computer observes it differently—through frames, body landmarks, coordinates, and timestamps.

This presentation explores how those observations could move through a Data Engineering pipeline, from raw data ingestion and validation to structured storage and SQL-based analysis.

### Concepts explored

- Computer vision and human pose estimation
- Coordinates, timestamps, and structured data
- Data ingestion, validation, cleaning, and transformation
- Relational data modeling and SQL
- Movement synchronization and the limitations of numerical measurements

### The bigger question

Technology can help us identify patterns in movement, but can it truly understand the emotions, intentions, and human connections behind a performance?

**Sometimes, what matters most cannot be reduced to numbers.**

### Project status

This repository documents a completed educational presentation and a conceptual Data Engineering workflow. The examples are illustrative; no actual pose-estimation pipeline was implemented on the referenced dance performance.

### Future possibilities

This concept may eventually evolve into a hands-on project involving pose-estimation data, an ETL pipeline, SQL analysis, and interactive visualizations.
